FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Kya loan ke liye CIBIL score hona zaroori hai?
Nahi, hum CIBIL score ko strictly check nahi karte. Aapke documents aur repayment capacity ke basis par loan approve kiya jaata hai.
Approval fees kya hai aur yeh kyun leni hai?
Approval fees ek one-time charge hai jo aapke loan application ko process karne, documents verify karne, aur loan amount disburse karne ke liye li jaati hai. Yeh amount aapke loan amount ke hisaab se decide hoti hai. Yeh hi ekmatra fee hai — koi aur hidden charge nahi hai.
Kitna loan mil sakta hai aur kitne time mein?
Aap ₹5,000 se ₹5,00,000 tak loan le sakte hain. Application submit karne ke baad 2-5 minutes mein approval mil jaata hai, aur approval fees pay karne ke baad 30-60 minutes mein aapke bank account mein credit ho jaata hai.
Kaun kaun se documents chahiye hote hain?
Sirf 3 cheezein chahiye: (1) Aadhaar Card, (2) PAN Card, (3) Selfie/Photo. Aur koi document nahi chahiye. Na income proof, na salary slip, na bank statement.
Kya main pehle apply kar ke baad mein fees pay kar sakta hoon?
Haan, pehle aap form fill karke apply karein. Documents verify hone ke baad aapko approval fees pay karne ka link milega. Fees pay karne ke baad hi loan amount aapke account mein bhej diya jaayega.
Kya main jald se jald loan repay kar sakta hoon?
Haan, aap bina kisi prepayment charges ke apna loan jaldi chuka sakte hain. Isse aapke future loan eligibility bhi improve hoti hai.
